# Flora Note 🌸
> Mindful Floriography & Custom Bouquet Studio

**Flora Note** is a contemplative online floral studio where customers can explore the deeper language of flowers (floriography), thoughtfully customize hand-tied bouquets with real-time visual previews, compose personalized handwritten greeting cards, and arrange local delivery in Phnom Penh.

## ✨ Key Features
1. **Meaning & Symbolism Catalog**: Explore flowers with their botanical names, floriography philosophies, and color variations.
2. **Interactive Bouquet Builder**:
   - Customize bouquet sizes: **Small**, **Medium**, and **Large**.
   - Select individual stems, quantities, and multi-color varieties (e.g., both peach and white tulips in one bouquet).
   - Choose artisan wrapping styles and silk ribbons.
   - Live, photorealistic hand-tied bouquet preview.
3. **Greeting Card Studio**: Compose personalized messages with handwritten typography and curated message templates.
4. **Phnom Penh Delivery & Review**: Full order review with district selection across Phnom Penh and simulated KHQR payment flow.
